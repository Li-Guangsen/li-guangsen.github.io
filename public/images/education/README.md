# Education image source

`sdust-official-logo.png` is the unmodified official university lockup from
[Shandong University of Science and Technology](https://www.sdust.edu.cn/images/logo.png).
The interface shows its circular seal through a CSS viewport, preserving the
original image and proportions.

The [university charter](https://www.sdust.edu.cn/xxgk/dxzc.htm) describes the
blue-and-white circular seal with its bird and technology-flower emblem.
The mark is used only to identify the school in the education section.
